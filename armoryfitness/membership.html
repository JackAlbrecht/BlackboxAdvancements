Before you sign up

How the paperwork works.

Short version: schedule a 15-minute appointment with a staff member. Bring cash for the first month and your $6 key fob. Sign the month-to-month paperwork. Auto-pay handles future months. No contract, no cancellation fee. 18+ to sign up; 16–17 can train during staff hours; 12–17 need a parent for sign-up.

Insurance & benefits accepted

Your plan might already cover us.

Armory accepts most major fitness benefits programs. If you have one of these, bring your card to your sign-up appointment and we’ll verify your benefits and set up access.

SS Silver Sneakers Covered through most Medicare Advantage plans. Bring your Silver Sneakers ID at sign-up. Accepted
A&F Active & Fit Through insurance or employer benefits. Reduced or fully-covered access, plan dependent. Accepted
RNW Renew / OnePass / Aaptiv All one program under different names depending on your provider. Same access — bring whichever card you have. Accepted
