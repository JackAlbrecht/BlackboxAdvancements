Since 2017 — Ferndale, WA

A gym for people who want the results, not the theater.

Armory Fitness is a 24-hour strength and recovery club on Main Street, Ferndale. Small enough to know your name. Serious enough to change your decade.

The Owner

Jackie Mills.

Jackie is a certified personal trainer, Nutritional Therapy Practitioner, and lifelong advocate for health and fitness. Her passion for wellness began at age five through deep involvement in sports. She holds her ACE Personal Training certification, her Nutritional Therapy Practitioner credential, plus specialized certifications in Sports Nutrition, Youth Athletes, and Behavioral Change.

In 2017, Jackie bought the gym where she now helps clients train for life. She understands the powerful connection between food and well-being — that for many, food itself can be an addiction. Her approach combines physical training with nutritional therapy, helping clients see that exercise isn’t just a choice. It’s a way of life.

“Everyone can and should find a form of exercise they love. With the right support, they can reach any goal they set.”
