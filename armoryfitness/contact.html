Service area

Whatcom County, most of it.

Our members commute from Ferndale, Bellingham, Lynden, Blaine, Birch Bay, Lummi Island and as far as Mount Vernon. If you’re within a 25-minute drive, you’ve found your gym.

From Ferndale

Heart of town

Right on Main Street. Free parking. Most Ferndale members are in and out in a 40-minute window before work.

From Bellingham

15 minutes up I-5

A lot of our Bellingham members come for the recovery wing — cold plunge and sauna combos that don’t exist at most boutique studios in Fairhaven or downtown.

From Lynden / Blaine

15–20 minutes

Easy access off Slater, Main, and Portal Way. 24/7 access makes early morning routes painless.
