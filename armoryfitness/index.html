The Philosophy

A real gym, with everything you actually need.

For too long, fitness has been sold as intimidation — polished showrooms, hidden contracts, pressure to perform. Armory is the opposite. A clean, 24-hour space built around the everyday athlete who just wants to get stronger, move better and live longer.

Full floor of premium strength, free weights, cardio and functional equipment. Train your way, on your schedule, 24 hours a day — with trainers on the floor when you want them.
