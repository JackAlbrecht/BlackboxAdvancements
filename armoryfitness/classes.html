Why the class package pays for itself

$75/month vs. $18 drop-ins.

Most boutique studios in Whatcom and Bellingham charge $18–28 per class. Armory’s unlimited class membership includes every strength class at a fixed $75/month — tax included — plus 24-hour gym access. Train four times a week and your effective per-class cost is under $5.

01

Real programming.

Weekly cycles with progressive overload. You’re not doing 47 burpees because the coach forgot to plan. You’re training a system.

02

Small groups.

You’re not a barcode. Coaches see your form, adjust your load, and know if you’re having a hard week.

03

Gym access bundled.

Every class membership includes 24-hour gym access. Train with the group on weekdays, alone on weekends — one price.
