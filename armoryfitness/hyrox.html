What’s coming

Three programs · one race standard.

If you don’t know what Hyrox is yet, you will. It’s the fastest-growing functional fitness racing format in the world — eight 1km runs alternated with eight workout stations. We’re training the whole pipeline.

01

Hyrox Foundations

For brand-new athletes. Learn every station, build base conditioning, and get comfortable with the format before you ever sign up for a race.

02

Hyrox Level 1 Training

Programmed weekly training for athletes prepping for a Hyrox event. Pacing, transitions, station efficiency, race-day mindset.

03

Race-Day Simulators

Full-format simulator sessions on the actual stations. Lock in your pacing, test your fueling, and walk into your event knowing exactly what to expect.
