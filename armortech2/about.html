The short version.

Armor Tech Resurfacing is a family-run resurfacing contractor based in Portland, Washington. We've been resurfacing homes and businesses across the Portland-Vancouver metro for over 20 years — interior, exterior, cabinets, commercial, drywall, and everything in between.

We're not the biggest outfit in town and we don't try to be. What we are is reliable: when we say we'll be there Monday at 8, we're there Monday at 8. When we say a job will take four days, it takes four days. When we give you a price, that's the price — no change-orders unless you change the scope.

That tagline has been on our trucks for 20 years. We do good services, we work with integrity, and we simplify a process most homeowners dread. Everything else is a detail.

Why Portland chose us.

Most of our work comes from referrals. A happy customer tells a neighbor. The neighbor tells their contractor. The contractor calls us for the next flip. That's been our growth model for 20 years — no paid ads, no lead-gen services, just good work that gets us recommended.

What we believe

  • Prep is the job. If the surface isn't ready, the paint won't hold. 80% of our time on a project is surface prep.
  • Quality paint pays for itself. We only use Sherwin-Williams and Benjamin Moore. Cheap paint costs twice as much over ten years.
  • Clean work sites. Your home doesn't stop being your home while we're in it.
  • One price. Our quote is our quote. No surprise fees.
  • Stand behind every job. If something fails within the warranty, we come back and fix it.

Service areas.

We serve Portland, Vancouver, Gresham, Beaverton, Hillsboro, Tigard, Lake Oswego, Oregon City, and the full Portland-Vancouver metro region. Outside our normal area? Still call — we'll tell you if we can fit it in.
