FAQ

How long does garage floor coatings take?

An average living room takes 1–2 days. A whole-house interior usually runs 5–10 working days depending on size and color count. We'll give you a timeline at the free estimate.

Do I have to move furniture?

No. We move furniture to the center of the room and cover it — no heavy lifting on your end.

Can you match my existing color?

Yes. Bring us a sample (a 2" square is plenty) and we'll match it at the paint store.

What does garage floor coatings cost in Portland?

Single rooms run ~$400–$800. Whole-home interior typically lands between $4,500 and $9,500 depending on size, ceilings, and trim. Use our instant estimator for a ballpark on your project.

Is your paint safe for kids and pets?

Yes. We default to low-VOC paints and can go zero-VOC on request. Rooms are safe to occupy within hours of the final coat.
