Basement Flake.

Basement floors don't have to be plain concrete or carpet. A light-color flake system gives you a clean, easy-care surface that handles spills, kids, dogs, and Idaho mud. We also seal against any moisture issues common in older basements.

Best for

Finished basements, rec rooms, home gyms, bonus rooms over slab.

Moisture testing

Older basements can have moisture issues. We do a free moisture test as part of every estimate before quoting.

Color options

Lighter blends are most popular - they brighten the space. Custom blends to match interior available.

Typical price

$6-$10 per square foot depending on slab condition and prep.
