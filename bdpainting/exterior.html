Exterior painting built for Yakima weather.

Yakima summers push 100°F and winters drop well below freezing. That kind of climate is brutal on exterior paint. B&D Painting preps and paints exteriors that hold up — with proper washing, scraping, priming, and two coats of weather-rated paint.

Soft-wash pressure cleaning, full scrape of loose paint, feathered sanding, caulking of gaps and joints, bare-wood priming, two full coats of exterior-grade paint, trim & doors included.

Exterior surfaces we paint

  • Wood siding & shake — the most common in Yakima's older neighborhoods
  • Stucco & EIFS — elastomeric coatings for hairline cracks
  • Hardie board & fiber cement — acrylic-latex holds for 12+ years
  • Metal siding & trim — rust-inhibiting primer + industrial topcoat
  • Brick & masonry — breathable mineral paint options
  • Decks & fences — stain, seal, or solid-color finish

When to repaint your exterior.

Most Yakima Valley homes need a full repaint every 7–12 years. Watch for: chalking (color comes off on your finger), peeling or flaking, fading on south and west walls, or exposed bare wood. If you're seeing any of those, don't wait — bare wood that gets rained on will rot.

FAQ

How long does exterior painting take?

Average 1,800–2,500 sqft home: 4–7 working days including prep. Larger homes or major prep work can run 8–12 days.

What does exterior painting cost in Yakima?

Most single-family homes run $3,500–$8,500. Large two-story or heavy-prep homes can reach $15,000+. Get a real range from our instant estimator.

Do you paint in the winter?

Only on warm, dry stretches above 50°F. We book most exterior work April through October.

Will the paint job peel in a few years?

Not if it's prepped right. Our exterior work typically holds 10–12 years on wood, 15+ on Hardie board.
