Cabinet refinishing that looks like new.

Replacing a kitchen costs $20,000+. Refinishing your existing cabinets runs a fraction of that — and done right, you can't tell the difference. B&D Painting sprays cabinet doors and drawers off-site in our shop so you get a factory-grade finish, then rebuilds and reinstalls everything with new hardware if you want.

1. Doors + drawers removed and labeled · 2. Taken to our shop for degreasing, sanding, priming · 3. Sprayed with cabinet-grade enamel (3 coats) · 4. Boxes painted in-home · 5. Reinstalled with new hinges if requested. Total time: 5–9 working days.

What we refinish

  • Kitchen cabinets — doors, drawers, boxes, side panels
  • Bathroom vanities
  • Built-ins & bookshelves
  • Kitchen islands (same or contrasting color)
  • Crown molding & cabinet trim

FAQ

What does cabinet refinishing cost?

Average 20-door kitchen runs $1,700–$3,000. Larger kitchens with islands can reach $4,500. Use our estimator for a specific range.

How long does the kitchen stay unusable?

You lose the cabinet doors for 5–7 days while they're in our shop. The boxes stay in place — you can still use the kitchen. Doors go back on once fully cured.

Will the paint chip or peel?

Not with proper prep and the right enamel. Our cabinet work typically holds 8–12+ years of daily kitchen use.

Can you change the color significantly?

Yes — even dark-to-white works with proper primer. We use a pigmented stain-blocking primer so wood tannins don't bleed through.
