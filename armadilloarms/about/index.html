Home/About

A single shop. A single standard.

Armadillo Arms & Machine runs out of one building in Ferndale, Washington — gunsmithing, Cerakote, engraving, and custom work done under one roof, under one name.

We do all our own work. If you drop a pistol, rifle, or shotgun on our counter for a trigger job, a red-dot cut, a Cerakote, or an NFA engraving, it doesn't leave the building. No referrals, no sub-contractors, no hand-offs.

That's not a brand claim — it's the operational reason we exist. Most coating and smithing shops outsource either the machine work or the finish work. Armadillo keeps both in-house because that's the only way the quality stays consistent.

We're a small shop. We quote honest turnarounds, communicate when something slips, and deliver work we'd put our own name on — because we do. Buy · Sell · Trade · Gunsmithing.
