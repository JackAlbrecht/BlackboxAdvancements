Rifle work at Armadillo ranges from an AR upper-and-lower assembly with headspace verification, to a ground-up precision build on a blueprinted action. The work that happens between those two ends is where most of our rifle customers live.

Barrel work is done on-bench: chambering to SAAMI or 'wildcat' specs, threading for suppressors or brakes, and crown work verified against reference. Every barrel job ships with a caliper-measured spec sheet so you know what you got.

Bedding and accuracy work is about eliminating variables. Pillars get installed straight, action screws torque to a consistent spec, and the bedding compound cures clamped against a known datum. The rifle goes out zeroed-in, documented, and ready for a load-development session — not a 'good luck' handshake.

How the work moves

The rifle gunsmithing process.

  1. 01

    Intake & confirm scope

    Paperwork, photos, scope confirmed in writing before we touch the work.

  2. 02

    Disassemble & inspect

    Full strip. Photographs. Anything wrong gets flagged before we proceed.

  3. 03

    On the bench

    Machining, fitting, or coating per spec. One operator, one piece at a time.

  4. 04

    Function test

    Every system gets a function / fitment verification before reassembly.

  5. 05

    Release

    Final QC, documentation, and a straight walk-through of what we touched.
