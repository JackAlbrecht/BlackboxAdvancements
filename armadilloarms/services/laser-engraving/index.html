Laser Engraving

Permanent, crisp, precision engraving — compliance-spec serial marking for NFA items, custom logos, dedications, and clean text on metal, polymer, and Cerakoted finishes.

Laser engraving at Armadillo covers two lanes: compliance work (NFA serial marking on suppressors and SBRs at the required depth and character-height spec) and personalization work (logos, dedications, names, and artwork).

We engrave at depth — not burn-surface — which means the mark is readable and will outlast the finish. On Cerakoted parts we engrave in a way that cuts through cleanly without creating a burn halo that ruins adjacent surface. On polymer we adjust the raster settings so the frame isn't damaged.

For NFA customers: the paperwork is your responsibility (we help you understand the process, not do it for you), but the engraving itself is compliance-correct on the first pass and documented with photos for your records.

Ideal for
  • ✓NFA serial engraving on suppressors + SBRs
  • ✓Custom logos + dedications on slides and receivers
  • ✓Compliance marking on builds requiring serial number
  • ✓Clean text through Cerakote without burn halo
Not a good fit
  • —Engraving on a firearm you do not have documented rights to mark
  • —Serial alteration on an existing compliance serial
How the work moves

The laser engraving process.

  1. 01

    Intake & confirm scope

    Paperwork, photos, scope confirmed in writing before we touch the work.

  2. 02

    Disassemble & inspect

    Full strip. Photographs. Anything wrong gets flagged before we proceed.

  3. 03

    On the bench

    Machining, fitting, or coating per spec. One operator, one piece at a time.

  4. 04

    Function test

    Every system gets a function / fitment verification before reassembly.

  5. 05

    Release

    Final QC, documentation, and a straight walk-through of what we touched.
