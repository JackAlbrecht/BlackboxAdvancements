Consignment

Let our shop move it.

Got a firearm you'd rather sell to the right buyer than argue about on a classifieds board? Drop it off with us. We list it, show it, and handle the transfer — you stay out of the back-and-forth.

  1. 01

    Bring it in

    Walk into the shop with a valid ID and the firearm you want to sell. We log it, photograph it, and write a consignment agreement on the spot.

  2. 02

    We list + market it

    We shoot it properly, spec it honestly, and list it across our channels — in-store, local buyers, and our online reach. No generic catalog cards.

  3. 03

    Compliant transfer

    Every sale runs through a full federal / state / local background check and transfer. You stay anonymous to the buyer. We handle the paperwork.

  4. 04

    You get paid

    Funds clear after the transfer closes. You pick check, card, or shop credit — most people turn it into the next rifle.

The process

Every job moves through the same bench.

Doesn't matter if it's a trigger job or a custom build — it goes through intake, prep, bench, cure, and QC. That's the work.

  1. 01

    Intake & spec

    Firearm in, paperwork logged, photos on file. We confirm scope, finish, and turnaround before anything is touched.

  2. 02

    Disassemble & prep

    Complete strip to the component level. Every surface is inspected, cleaned, and prepared the way the end-state demands.

  3. 03

    Machine · Coat · Engrave

    Gunsmithing, Cerakote application, or laser engraving — whichever the job calls for, performed on-bench and verified against spec.

  4. 04

    Cure & reassemble

    Oven-cured to the coating's spec. Reassembled with the original fitment, function-checked, and re-verified.

  5. 05

    Final check & release

    Function test, visual QC, and documentation. You get the work — and a straight-answer walkthrough of everything we touched.
