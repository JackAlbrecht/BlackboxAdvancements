Good questions

Answers before you ask.

The questions we hear on almost every walkthrough — answered straight.

How long does a garage floor take to install?+

Most residential garages are a single-day install. We grind, repair, base-coat, broadcast flake or metallic, and topcoat with polyaspartic — all between breakfast and dinner. You can walk on the floor that night and park a car on it within 24 hours.

Why do you diamond-grind instead of acid-etch?+

Acid-etching is what big-box-store kits tell you to do. It leaves residue, it's impossible to flush completely, and the coating fails at the concrete interface in 12–36 months. Diamond-grinding gets the slab down to clean, open concrete — the only prep method where polyaspartic can bond mechanically and last 20+ years.

Do you cover Mt Hood and the gorge?+

Yes. Boring sits on Highway 26, so we're the natural shop for everything from Troutdale and Corbett out to Government Camp. Mt Hood freeze-thaw is why we use polyaspartic — it's rated down to −40°F and doesn't brittle-fail when your cabin goes unheated in January.

What's the warranty?+

10-year written warranty on residential, 5-year on commercial. Covers moisture delamination, hot-tire peel, and UV chalking. The floor itself is built to outlive the warranty — it's just the paperwork.

How much does it cost?+

Fair-market Portland-metro pricing. A typical 2-car garage runs $2,800–$5,200 depending on system (flake is cheaper, metallic is higher). Shops and commercial spaces are quoted by the square foot plus any slab-repair scope. Our in-person walkthroughs are always free — and we'll tell you if your slab isn't a candidate before we quote.

Do you take on commercial jobs?+

Yes — warehouses, shops, restaurants, retail, auto bays, fleet bays. We schedule off-hours installs so you don't lose a working day. 5-year commercial warranty. Licensed and insured in Oregon under OR CCB #250294.
