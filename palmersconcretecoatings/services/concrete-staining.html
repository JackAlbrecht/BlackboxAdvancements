Concrete Staining

Concrete stain gives you depth and color variation that paint or epoxy cannot. The stain reacts with the concrete itself, so the color becomes part of the slab. Perfect for patios, basements, living rooms, and commercial spaces where you want a natural, earth-tone look.

Acid stain vs water-based

Acid stain reacts chemically with the concrete for rich earth tones (amber, terra cotta, forest). Water-based stain offers a wider palette including blues, grays, and soft neutrals.

Indoor or outdoor

Stain works on both. Exterior slabs get a UV-stable sealer on top to lock in the color and protect against weather.

Pattern options

Solid stain, variegated, stenciled borders, scored tile patterns, faux-slate. We will walk you through samples at the estimate.

Typical price range

$3 to $7 per square foot depending on pattern and sealer.
