If you can see your landscaping through your roof, you do not have a small roofing issue. You have a major structural failure.

The image above shows a hole completely through the roof decking. You can literally see the greenery below through the sheathing. This did not happen overnight. Roof failures like this are the result of long term moisture intrusion, trapped debris, neglected maintenance, and delayed repairs.

This is exactly why SRS Roofing urges homeowners in Bellingham, Lynden, Ferndale, Blaine, and throughout Whatcom County to address roof problems early.

In the Pacific Northwest, moisture is constant. Rain, wind driven storms, moss growth, clogged gutters, and poor ventilation all work together against your roof.

Here is how small problems turn into major failures:

• Debris builds up and traps moisture

• Moss holds water against shingles

• Water seeps under aging or damaged shingles

• Roof decking absorbs moisture

• Wood begins to rot• Structural integrity weakens

• Eventually, the decking fails completely

By the time you see a hole like this, the damage has been developing for years.

Many homeowners delay roof replacement because they hope a small leak can wait. Unfortunately, roof issues never fix themselves. They always get worse.

A minor repair can turn into:

• Interior drywall damage

• Electrical hazards• Structural framing deterioration

At that point, you are no longer paying for a roof repair. You are paying for structural restoration.

Early roof replacement or professional roof repair from SRS Roofing is almost always far less expensive than waiting.

You do not need to wait until you can see daylight through your roof. Call SRS Roofing if you notice:

• Soft or spongy areas when walking on the roof

• Persistent moss growth

• Missing, curling, or cracked shingles

• Granules collecting in gutters

• Water stains on ceilings

• Roof age over 18 to 25 years
