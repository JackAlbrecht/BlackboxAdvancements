Nooksack — residential shingle install

New residential roof install with architectural shingles and upgraded flashing at every chimney and skylight transition.

Nooksack, WA · Whatcom County 3 working days Residential
1 / 5

Focus on the weak points

Four skylights and a chimney — the top four leak-source categories in Washington. Every one got brand-new flashing, ice-and-water shield wrap, and a proper cricket behind the chimney.

The overlooked detail

The previous roof had been installed without step flashing at the left dormer wall — just sealant. We cut into the siding, installed proper step flashing tied into the underlayment, and resealed the siding behind the flashing. Will not leak again.

Materials used
  • Architectural shingle (CertainTeed Landmark)
  • Full ice-and-water shield valleys + chimney wrap
  • New step flashing at all wall transitions
  • Custom-cut chimney cricket
