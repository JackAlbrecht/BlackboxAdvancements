Sudden Valley — cedar to composition conversion

Converted a century-old cedar shake roof to designer composition shingle with a full fire-upgrade detailing package — HOA-approved color match.

Sudden Valley, WA · Whatcom County 5 working days Material swap
1 / 5

Why swap cedar

Cedar shake had rotted on the north-facing slopes (typical for Sudden Valley's tree canopy and moisture load). Homeowners were tired of the maintenance cycle and wanted something that would last 40 years with minimal upkeep.

HOA-friendly aesthetic

Sudden Valley HOA has strong aesthetic guidelines — we spec'd a CertainTeed Presidential Shake TL in Autumn Blend that reads as cedar from the curb but carries the fire rating and warranty of modern composition.

Fire upgrade details

Upgraded underlayment to Class A fire-rated, full ice-and-water shield, stainless step flashing at all wall transitions, and non-combustible drip edge. The new roof system meets WUI (wildland-urban interface) code for the area.
