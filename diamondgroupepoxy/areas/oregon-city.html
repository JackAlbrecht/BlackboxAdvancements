Why Oregon City

Built for the way Oregon City lives.

Oregon City has some of the most interesting slabs we encounter anywhere in the metro. Between the historic homes on the bluff above the Willamette, the mid-century stock throughout Gaffney Lane, and the newer construction out toward Beavercreek, we see garages from almost every era of Oregon construction history. Older Oregon City slabs in particular often have moisture issues and hairline cracking that a diamond-grind-and-rebuild system is uniquely suited to fix. We've turned plenty of 1960s Oregon City garages from cracked, oil-stained, hopeless-looking concrete into seamless, warrantied floors in a single working day. Oregon City is roughly 30 minutes from our shop and a regular part of our weekly route.

  • Historic bluff-top homes above the Willamette
  • Gaffney Lane mid-century residential garages
  • Beavercreek-corridor newer construction
