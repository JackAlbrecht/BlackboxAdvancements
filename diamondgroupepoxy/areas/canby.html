Why Canby

Built for the way Canby lives.

Canby is farm country with teeth, and the floors we install here reflect it. Canby garages are often attached to serious shop buildings — hobby farms, classic-car projects, small-ag operations off 99E, landscaping outfits running equipment out of outbuildings. Those floors see mud, fuel, fertilizer, and hydraulic fluid. A thin acrylic sealer from the hardware store will not survive a single Oregon winter under those conditions. Our polyaspartic and cement-urethane systems will. We also do a lot of residential work in the newer Canby subdivisions south of Territorial Road where owners want a clean, finished look for their primary garage. Canby is a 40-minute drive from our Beaverton shop and worth every mile.

  • Hobby-farm shop buildings and outbuildings off 99E
  • Territorial Road residential subdivisions
  • Small-ag and landscaping fleet garages

Heavy-duty shop-floor systems plus residential flake and metallic throughout Canby and the southern metro.
