About this system

Solid Color Epoxy Floors, done right.

Sometimes you don't want flake, you don't want metallic swirls, and you don't want the raw concrete look. You want a clean, uniform, solid-color floor — battleship gray, tile red, safety yellow, or a custom-matched shade that ties into your cabinets or brand palette. We install solid-color systems in exactly the same way we install our premium flake floors: full diamond grind, 100%-solids pigmented epoxy basecoat, and a two-coat polyaspartic topcoat. The only difference is what goes in the middle.

Solid-color epoxy is the right call for several specific use cases. It's the traditional finish for industrial and commercial shop floors where line striping matters and high contrast helps with safety. It's popular in home gyms where a consistent color backdrop makes the space feel more like a professional facility. And it's increasingly requested in modern-build Portland garages where homeowners want the minimalist look of a continuous monochrome floor instead of a textured flake pattern. We can mix to any standard color code — OSHA safety colors, Pantone, Sherwin-Williams, whatever you need.

Durability is identical to our flake system. The finished floor is fully waterproof, chemical-resistant, hot-tire proof, and backed by the same 10-year residential or 5-year commercial warranty. The one tradeoff to know: solid colors show dust and scratches slightly more than flake does, because there's no pattern to break up the eye. For high-traffic homes we recommend a satin or matte polyaspartic finish — it hides normal wear without sacrificing performance. For showrooms and shops we go high-gloss. Either way, the floor goes in during a single working day.

FAQ

Solid Color Epoxy Floors questions.

Will the color fade in sunlight?

No. Our polyaspartic topcoat is UV-stable, which keeps the pigment below from breaking down even under direct sun. Solid colors hold their hue for the full life of the floor — a real concern with cheaper epoxy-only systems that tend to yellow and fade.

Can you match a specific brand color?

Yes. Bring us a swatch, a paint code, or even a product sample and we'll color-match the pigment. We routinely match Sherwin-Williams, Benjamin Moore, and brand-specific safety colors for commercial clients.
