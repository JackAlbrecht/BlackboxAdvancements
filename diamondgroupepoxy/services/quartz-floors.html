About this system

Quartz Epoxy Floors, done right.

Quartz systems are the workhorse of commercial flooring. We install a double-broadcast of colored quartz aggregate into two separate epoxy basecoats, then seal the stack under a chemical-resistant urethane or polyaspartic topcoat. The finished floor is thick — typically 3/16-inch built up — completely seamless, and extremely slip-resistant even when wet. That combination is why you'll find our quartz installs in commercial kitchens from Beaverton to Gresham, craft breweries in inner Southeast Portland, dog daycares in Tualatin, and lab spaces along the Hillsboro tech corridor.

The advantage of quartz over flake in a commercial setting comes down to thickness, aggregate hardness, and cleanability. Colored quartz is a mined mineral with a Mohs hardness of 7 — harder than most things that will ever roll across it. The double broadcast builds up a dense, puncture-resistant wear layer that shrugs off dropped tools, pallet corners, and heavy rolling loads. And because the entire system is monolithic and cove-able up the wall, there are no grout lines, no seams, and nowhere for bacteria, grease, or standing water to hide. Health inspectors love it. So do the people who have to mop at 2 a.m.

We install quartz in both standard colors and custom blends. For wet kitchens we recommend a textured urethane topcoat that holds an R10-plus slip rating even under grease. For clean rooms and lab spaces we run a smoother polyaspartic that still cleans aggressively but doesn't grab debris. Either way, we finish in phases so your business can keep operating, and we back the install with a 5-year commercial warranty.

FAQ

Quartz Epoxy Floors questions.

Is quartz slippery when wet?

No. The broadcast aggregate creates built-in texture that stays grippy even under grease and water. We can dial the aggressiveness up or down depending on your space — kitchen floors get more bite, lab floors get less.

How long do I need to close?

Most quartz installs take two to three working days. We can work overnight or phase the install room-by-room so you stay open during business hours.
