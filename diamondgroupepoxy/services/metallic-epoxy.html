About this system

Metallic Epoxy Floors, done right.

Metallic epoxy is a statement floor. We blend reflective mica pigments into a clear 100%-solids epoxy, pour it onto your prepared slab, and hand-work the material with solvent, torches, and rollers to create swirls, lenses, and three-dimensional depth that no two floors ever share. Every metallic we install is one of a kind. Homeowners in the West Hills use it in wine cellars and basement bars. Beaverton dealerships use it on showroom floors. Tigard med-spas use it in lobbies. Anywhere the floor is part of the experience, metallic earns its keep.

The system is more than eye candy. Underneath the artistic topcoat is a full epoxy build — diamond-ground concrete, a pigmented basecoat, the metallic pour, and two coats of high-gloss polyaspartic on top. That stack is fully waterproof, chemically resistant, easy to mop, and rated for showroom and light commercial traffic. It handles dropped glassware, spilled wine, and a decade of foot traffic without yellowing, chalking, or losing its mirror gloss. Because polyaspartic is UV-stable, the floor holds its color even under big south-facing windows — a real concern in Portland homes with modern glass walls.

Metallic is a craft install. We spend time with you picking pigments, mocking up sample boards, and walking through the space so the finished floor matches the light, the cabinetry, and the vibe you're after. Lead times run slightly longer than a flake install because of the sample process, but the floor itself still goes in over a single working day. Pricing varies with color count and square footage — call us for a quick in-home quote.

FAQ

Metallic Epoxy Floors questions.

Will the gloss dull over time?

Not for a long time. Our polyaspartic topcoat is engineered for showroom use and holds its gloss for 10-plus years under normal interior traffic. A simple mop-up is all the maintenance most floors ever need.

Can I pick the colors?

Absolutely. We carry dozens of mica pigments and will build sample boards in your home or shop before we install. You sign off on the exact palette before we pour a drop.
