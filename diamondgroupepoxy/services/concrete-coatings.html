About this system

Concrete Coatings Portland, done right.

Concrete coating is a catch-all phrase for any high-performance system that seals and protects a slab. At Diamond Group we specialize in polyaspartic coatings — a next-generation chemistry that cures faster than epoxy, resists UV yellowing, and comes out roughly four times stronger in abrasion tests. That combination is why we use polyaspartic as the topcoat on nearly every floor we install, and why we recommend it as a standalone system for covered patios, sunrooms, back porches, and any other slab that sees direct sunlight.

A typical concrete coating project starts with a full diamond grind. We open the pores of the slab with industrial grinders and HEPA-filtered vacuums — no dust plume in your house, no acid wash running down the driveway. We chase every crack and spall with a polyurea repair compound, re-grind the patches flat, and prime the whole slab. From there we either broadcast flake, pour a solid color, or run a neat two-coat polyaspartic depending on the look you want. Because polyaspartic recoats in about two hours, the entire system finishes in a single working day and is ready for foot traffic that evening.

Polyaspartic outperforms old-school epoxy in almost every way that matters in Oregon. It won't yellow under the long summer sun. It stays flexible through winter freeze-thaw cycles. It shrugs off road salt, de-icer, gasoline, and brake fluid. And because it bonds chemically to the primed slab, it won't peel, bubble, or delaminate the way DIY kits from the big-box stores always eventually do. If you've been burned by a cheap coating in the past, this is the fix.

FAQ

Concrete Coatings Portland questions.

How is this different from epoxy?

Epoxy is an older chemistry that cures slowly and yellows under UV. Polyaspartic cures in hours, stays color-stable in sunlight, and tests about four times stronger in abrasion. It costs a little more per gallon but lasts significantly longer.

Will it peel?

Not when it's installed correctly. Peeling happens when a coating is applied over a dirty or acid-etched slab. We diamond-grind every floor to clean, porous concrete — which is the only prep method that guarantees a permanent mechanical bond.
