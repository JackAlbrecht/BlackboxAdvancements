About this system

Commercial & Industrial Epoxy Floors, done right.

Commercial and industrial flooring isn't a bigger version of a residential garage job — it's a different discipline. We've spent years installing resinous systems in warehouses, production lines, breweries, auto shops, distribution centers, and light-manufacturing spaces across the Portland metro and Southwest Washington. Every commercial project starts with a site walk, a moisture test, a service-load discussion, and a written spec that ties the right system to the right use case. A brewery needs thermal-shock-rated urethane. A forklift-heavy warehouse needs a thick quartz buildup. A machine shop needs chemical resistance. We won't sell you a floor that's wrong for the job, and we won't undersell one that won't survive.

Operationally, we install around your schedule, not ours. We run weekend crews, overnight shifts, and phased room-by-room installs so production never fully stops. We set up HEPA-filtered negative-pressure containment so dust doesn't migrate into clean areas or adjacent tenants. We coordinate with facility managers, GCs, and food-safety auditors. And every commercial install is documented with thickness measurements, slab moisture readings, batch records of every material applied, and a final walk-through punch list signed by your operations lead. That documentation matters at audit time.

Systems we install commercially include full quartz broadcast, double-broadcast flake, metallic showroom floors, solid-color industrial coatings, cement urethane, trench-drain integration, and slip-rated textured topcoats. We carry $2M in liability coverage, full workers comp, and every certification required to work in food-service and regulated environments. References on request — we'd rather have you call three of our past commercial clients than read another webpage of marketing copy.

FAQ

Commercial & Industrial Epoxy Floors questions.

Can you install while we stay open?

Almost always. We run overnight and weekend crews, phase the install room-by-room, and use fast-curing polyaspartic or cement urethane systems that let you walk on the new floor within hours. We'll build a schedule around your production calendar.

Do you handle the disposal and environmental side?

Yes. We capture grind dust with HEPA-filtered vacuums, contain any surface contaminants, and dispose of waste through licensed channels. For regulated facilities we provide full material data sheets and install documentation for your records.
