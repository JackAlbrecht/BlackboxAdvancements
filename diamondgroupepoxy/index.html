Our four-step install

Every floor, every time.

Diamond-grind

We strip every slab down to clean, open concrete with industrial diamond grinders. No acid etching, no shortcuts.

Repair & prime

Cracks, pits, and control joints get filled with structural polyurea. A moisture-mitigating primer locks everything down.

Color & flake

Pigmented basecoat goes down wet. We broadcast flake (or pour metallic) until the floor literally can't hold any more.

Polyaspartic seal

Two coats of UV-stable, hot-tire-proof polyaspartic. Walk on it that night, park on it in 24 hours.
