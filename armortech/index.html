// How it works

Four steps.
No surprises.

Same crew, start to finish
Cleaned up before we leave

Honest. Precise. Done when we say.

Every project runs the same way: an on-site walk-through, full prep, coatings applied by manufacturer-trained installers, and a final walk-through where we don't leave until you're happy.

Step 01

Free On-Site Estimate

We walk the project in person, talk scope and options, and email you a line-item quote within 48 hours. No pressure. No fees.

Step 02

Diamond Grind + Prep

Diamond grinding on garage floors. Acid etching on tubs. Cracks patched, moisture tested, surfaces sanitized. 80% of the job is in the prep.

Step 03

Coat + Broadcast

Base coat down, decorative flake or metallic broadcast, polyaspartic topcoat. Two-component systems that cure in hours, not days.

Step 04

Walkthrough + Warranty

Final inspection with you on-site. Touch-ups same day. Leftover kit handed off. Written lifetime warranty signed before we leave.
