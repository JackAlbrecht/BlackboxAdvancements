The Footage Problem

Most photo and video work ages in a year.
Some of it ages in a week.

You've hired the crew. They show up at noon, shoot through golden hour, pack the truck before the sky has finished going blue. The deliverables come back two weeks later. They look fine. They also look exactly like the deliverables your competitor got from the agency down the street last spring.

The cost of that shoot wasn't the day rate. The cost was eighteen months later, when the work felt dated and the brand had to reshoot. You paid twice for a thing you should have only paid for once.

Inventory footage is cheap because it's interchangeable. Footage that isn't interchangeable costs more upfront and less over the long run. That's the trade.

We shoot the way that doesn't age.
