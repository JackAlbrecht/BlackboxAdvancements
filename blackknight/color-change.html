From factory boring
to show-stopper.

A color change is more than a coat of paint — it's the moment your car becomes your car. Black Knight has been mixing one-off colors and custom finishes in Surrey for decades. Whether you want to flip your daily to satin black, drop a candy apple over your build, or chase a hard-to-mix OEM color, we'll spray it right.

Strip to substrate where needed, full sand & primer, color-matched base in your new color, 3 coats of clear, full jamb & trunk lid color change, machine polish, and re-install of trim & emblems.

Color change finishes we run

  • Solid candy reds, oranges & blues — depth you can fall into
  • Pearl whites & pearl blacks — flips color in the sun
  • Satin & matte — black, charcoal, military green, & OEM-matched satins

  • Tri-stage Kandy Kolor — House of Kolor mixes for show builds
  • Color shift / chameleon — multi-angle pigment for one-of-one looks

Why a color change isn't a single coat.

Skipping the jambs, gas door, hood underside or door shut faces is what separates a backyard color change from a real one. We take your car apart, paint everywhere paint should be, and reassemble it so the new color shows everywhere — even when the doors are open.

FAQ

How much does a color change cost?

Partial color change starts around $1,500. Full exterior runs $3,500–$6,500. Full color change including jambs & engine bay typically lands $6,500–$11,000. Premium candy & tri-stage finishes start at $11,000+.

How long will it take?

Full color change is typically 2–4 weeks in the booth. Premium tri-stage and custom mixes can run 4–6 weeks.

Can I bring my own color sample?

Yes. A chip, a swatch, or even a photo of a car you saw — we'll match it.

Do you re-register the new color with ICBC?

We provide the paint code & documentation so you can update your vehicle record with ICBC after pickup.
