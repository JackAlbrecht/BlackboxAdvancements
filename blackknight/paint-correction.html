Mirror finish.
No repaint.

Most "tired" paint isn't tired at all — it's covered in swirl marks, road haze, oxidation, and dealer wash damage hiding the gloss underneath. Multi-stage paint correction wet-sands and machine polishes the clear coat back to factory glass, then seals it for protection. Cheaper than a repaint, half the downtime, mirror finish.

Two-bucket wash, iron decon, clay bar, paint-thickness reading, masking, compound cut, polish, finishing pass on every panel, then a ceramic prep wipe so your paint is ready for sealant or coating.

Correction levels

  • One-stage enhancement — light swirl & haze removal, gloss boost
  • Two-stage correction — deeper swirl & light scratch removal, daily-driver glass
  • Three-stage / show prep — wet-sand pull-out, full RDS removal, concours-level finish
  • Ceramic prep — full panel wipe ready for ceramic coat or paint sealant

Why machine polish beats hand glaze.

Hand wax fills swirls. Machine polish removes them. Black Knight runs Rupes & Flex polishers with cut, polish, and finishing pads matched to your paint hardness. We also take paint thickness readings before any cut — so we never burn through clear.

FAQ

How long does paint correction take?

One-stage enhancement is a 1-day job. Two-stage runs 1–2 days. Three-stage show prep is 2–4 days depending on size and condition.

Will it remove every scratch?

Anything that didn't cut through the clear coat — yes. Deep scratches into base or primer need spot paint.

How long does the result last?

Bare polish lasts 2–4 months. Sealed with paint sealant: 6–12 months. Ceramic-coated: 2–5 years.

What does it cost?

Single-panel polish: $300–$600. Full-car two-stage: $800–$1,800. Show-quality three-stage with ceramic: $3,500+.
