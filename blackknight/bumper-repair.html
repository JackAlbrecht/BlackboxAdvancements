Texture matching

The hardest part of bumper repair is making it disappear. A patch that looks obviously smoother than the wall around it is a failed repair. We match your existing texture — knockdown, orange peel, skip-trowel, sand-finish, or smooth — so the fix blends in once painted.

Popcorn removal + ceiling refinish

Popcorn ceilings from the 70s and 80s are still everywhere in Surrey. We scrape them off, re-texture to modern smooth or knockdown, and paint in the same visit. (Asbestos testing on request for pre-1980 homes.)

FAQ

What does bumper repair cost?

Small patches start around $200. Full-room repairs run $550–$1,200. Extensive water-damage repairs can reach $2,800+. Use our estimator for a range.

How long for a bumper repair + paint?

Patches: same day. Full rooms: 2–3 days including texture drying time and paint cure.

Can you remove popcorn ceilings?

Yes. Scraped, skim-coated, re-textured, and painted — usually 2–4 days per room.
