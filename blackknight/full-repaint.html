Full vehicle repaints, done in the booth.

A factory paint job leaves a lot on the table. Color depth, panel match, jamb finish, clear coat thickness — all of it suffers when paint is sprayed on an assembly line. A real repaint, done by hand in a real booth, is how you take a car from "fine" to "stops people in parking lots." That's what Black Knight has been doing in Surrey for 35+ years.

Disassembly of trim & emblems, full strip-to-substrate sand, dent & chip repair, epoxy primer, high-build sand, three coats of base, three coats of clear, full bake & cure, color-sand and machine polish, then reinstall and final detail. Jambs, hood underside & trunk lid included on every full repaint.

Vehicles we paint most

  • Daily drivers — sedans, SUVs, trucks needing a factory-quality refresh
  • Resto projects — classic, vintage & muscle cars getting the right color, the right way

  • Lifted & off-road trucks — full repaints with bedliner accents and roll pans

Paint we trust.

Black Knight runs PPG, Axalta and House of Kolor through every booth job. Single-stage, base/clear, tri-stage candy, and Kandy Kolor all on the menu. We mix on-site, color-match against your panel under spray-booth lights, and spray with high-volume low-pressure (HVLP) guns for atomization that no driveway shop can touch.

FAQ

How long does a full vehicle repaint take?

A standard daily-driver repaint runs 2–3 weeks in the booth from drop-off to pickup. Resto projects with bodywork can take 4–8 weeks. We give you a panel-by-panel timeline at the free estimate.

Do you paint the engine bay and door jambs?

Jambs and trunk shut faces are included on every full repaint. Engine bay paint is an add-on if you want a show-quality result — most full restos include it.

Can you match my existing factory color?

Yes. We pull your factory color code, mix to it, then verify the match against your panel under spray-booth lights so the new paint reads identical to OEM.

What does a full repaint cost in Surrey?

A daily-driver in good condition starts around $4,500. Mid-tier with prep work runs $6,500–$10,000. Show-quality resto builds run $12,000+. Use our instant estimator for a ballpark on your build.

Will the new paint last?

Yes — booth-applied PPG / Axalta clear coat with proper cure outlasts factory paint. We back every full repaint with a written warranty against peel, fade, and clear failure.
