FAQ

How fast is fleet turnaround?

Single-vehicle repaint: 5–7 working days. Multi-truck batches: we stage them so you always have at least 75% of the fleet on the road. Most clients run 2–3 trucks at a time through our booth.

Do you handle wraps too?

We handle paint. For vinyl wraps we partner with two trusted local installers and can coordinate the full project so you have one point of contact.

What about ICBC fleet claims?

We work directly with ICBC fleet claims adjusters and can manage paint & bodywork through your insurer.

How is fleet pricing structured?

Single vehicles run $3,500–$8,000 depending on size. Multi-truck programs get volume pricing — typically 15–25% off list. Use our instant estimator for ballpark numbers.
