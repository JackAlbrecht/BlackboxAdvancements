Spot-perfect touch-up.

Not every blemish needs a full-panel repaint. A skilled spot repair — color matched, blended, and cleared into the surrounding paint — disappears. Rock chips, parking-lot scrapes, scratches deep enough to feel, key marks, and small bumper rubs all get fixed in a day or two without the cost or downtime of a full panel repaint.

Rock chip fill, single-panel scratch repair, bumper scuff repair, key-mark fill, mirror cap respray, headlight restoration, plastic trim repaint, and small dent paintless-pull where we can.

What we fix most

  • Rock chips on hoods, fenders & bumpers
  • Parking-lot scratches & scuffs — door dings, panel rubs
  • Key marks — long, deep scratches from kids/keys/carts
  • Bumper paint pull-off — corner taps, plastic stress cracks
  • Door edge chips — from opening into walls or other doors
  • Faded mirror caps — clear coat failure on plastic

Color blending, not just dabbing.

A dollar-store touch-up pen sits on top of your paint and stands out. Real spot repair pulls the new color into the existing clear coat with a feathered edge, so light reflects across the boundary and you can't see where new ends and old begins. That's what we do.

FAQ

How fast is a touch-up?

Rock chips and small scratches: same-day or next-day. Bumper scuffs: 1–2 days. Multi-panel spot repair: 2–4 days.

Will the touch-up be invisible?

On most modern paint, yes — under normal viewing conditions. Show-car level inspection (24" away, sunlight) may still reveal the spot. We'll be straight with you up front.

How much does touch-up cost?

Rock chip fill: $80–$200. Spot scratch: $200–$500. Single-panel respray: $500–$1,200. Multi-spot: $1,200–$2,500.
