Our story

Three generations. One little flower shop.

Over 67 years ago, Clary Jensen stood at a crossroads — become a farmer, or open his own floral business. He chose flowers because, as he puts it, "the floral business would have a better smell."

He opened Jensen's Ferndale Floral in 1957 on Third Avenue after completing floral school in Oregon. A few years later he moved the shop to Vista Drive, and in 1963 he married Rosalie, who quickly became what Clary still calls "the boss."

Together they raised three children in the shop, and for over six decades they've walked alongside this community — parades, weddings, funerals, birthdays, anniversaries, Pioneer Park festivities, big days and quiet ones. The full circle of life, one arrangement at a time.

Today the shop is still family-run and still on Vista Drive. Clary and Rosalie still visit, tend their garden and their chickens, and love hearing from the friends they've made over the years.

— Cheers to 67, Clary & Rosalie.
