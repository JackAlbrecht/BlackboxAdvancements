About JEKK Cards

Built by collectors, for collectors. From a single glass case in 2021 to 10 million cards in 2026.

OUR STORY

A shop built the right way.

JEKK Cards started with one glass case, a Ferndale garage, and a belief that card shops had quietly become hostile to the people who actually love the hobby. Opaque pricing. Back-room valuations. Singles you could never find.

We set out to fix that. Five years later, JEKK is the Pacific Northwest's biggest card shop — a 100-foot wax wall, a 1.5 million-card dig wall, live group breaks streamed six nights a week, and a community of collectors from Seattle to Vancouver who drive hours to walk through the door.

We still price off live comps. We still look you in the eye when we buy your collection. And we still believe the best card shops are the ones you hang out in, not the ones that just take your money.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Values baked into every single.

01

Fair Comps, Always

Every single is priced to live eBay and Beckett comps. No markup games. If it's cheaper online, match it — we'd rather keep the collector than the margin.

02

Hobby First, Margin Second

We break cases we could hoard. We buy collections fair even when the seller doesn't know values. The hobby is bigger than any one sale.

03

Community Counts

League nights, trade meets, launch parties, kids-welcome policy. A card shop is a room full of people. We act like it.

04

Transparency on Breaks

Every spot, every hit, every filler on camera. Shipping tracked. Hits listed publicly with photos. No chargebacks, no "missing cards."

05

Kids & Newcomers Welcome

Saturday afternoon, a $5 repack for the kid in the Seahawks jersey, a starter deck demo for the college student. Everyone starts somewhere.

06

The Hunt Is The Thing

We keep the dig wall deep, the singles organized, the repacks fresh. Because the best memories in this hobby are the ones you dig out yourself.
