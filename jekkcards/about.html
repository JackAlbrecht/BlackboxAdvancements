// Who we are

About
JEKK.

Forty-plus jerseys on the wall. Six break tables. A back room stacked with singles. The Pacific Northwest card shop you wished existed when you were twelve.

JEKK Cards is the card shop you wished existed when you were twelve — and this time it actually does. We're a full-line hobby shop in Bellingham, Washington, stocking sports cards, Pokémon, TCGs, and every major wax box that drops.

The shop is built around three things: the jersey wall — forty-plus game-worn and autographed jerseys across the NBA, NFL, MLB, and NHL; the break floor — six tables we run live breaks on most nights of the week; and the singles vault — rows of sorted singles, slabs, and bulk that customers actually want to dig through.

The shop, in photos.

What we do.

  • Sell wax — every major sport + TCG release, hobby and retail, current and carryover
  • Run live breaks — random team, hit draft, personal, most nights of the week
  • Grade cards — PSA and SGC submissions from the counter; we handle the whole process
  • Buy collections — cash offers on collections big and small, fair and transparent
  • Ship online — shop online at jekkcards.com if you can't visit

This is a real shop run by real collectors. We rip our own wax. We chase our own hits. We're not a reseller flipping sealed out of a warehouse — we're a room with six break tables, a jersey wall, and a back office stacked with singles, run by people who love the hobby.

Come visit.

179 Telegraph Rd, Bellingham WA 98226. Open 7 days a week, 11am – 7pm. Parking out front. Walk in, grab a pack, pull up a chair at a break table. You don't need to buy anything to say hi.
