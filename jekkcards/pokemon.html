Pokémon
the card shop

From 151 to Prismatic Evolutions, Japanese-exclusive sets to vintage WOTC, ETBs for the kids and sealed cases for the long-term hold — we stock it all at 179 Telegraph Rd.

Pokémon Wax · Sealed Boxes · Singles & Slabs · Group Breaks · PSA Grading · Buy · Sell · Trade ·

If you grew up on Pokémon, this is the room you walked into in 1999 and never left. Every current set on the shelf. Booster boxes, Elite Trainer Boxes, Collection Boxes, tins. A full singles case with PSA slabs, vintage holos, and alt-art chase cards.

We also carry Japanese-exclusive releases — 151, Terastal Festival, Shiny Treasure, Crimson Haze. And yes, we buy collections. Big or small, binder or bin, bring it in.

Current English + Japanese sealed, vintage singles, graded slabs, ETB walls, PSA slabs, personal breaks. We buy, sell, trade, and grade.

What we stock.

01

Sealed Wax

Current set booster boxes, ETBs, collection boxes, and tins. Japanese and English. We restock fast.

02

Singles & Slabs

Case of PSA-graded vintage, modern chase cards, full-art trainers, and pull-hunt favorites.

03

Personal Breaks

We rip a box for you on video and mail the whole thing back. Perfect when you want the hits, not the floor of bulk.

Brands on the shelf

  • The Pokémon Company International (all current English sets)
  • Japanese Pokémon TCG imports — 151, Crimson Haze, Shiny Treasure, Terastal Festival
  • WOTC Base, Jungle, Fossil, Team Rocket (vintage)
  • Elite Trainer Boxes, Collection Boxes, Premium Collections
  • Tins, Blisters, Build & Battle boxes

Rip it at the table.

Don't want to buy a whole box? Get in on a group break — we're running breaks most nights of the week. Pay for a spot, we rip the wax on camera, and you get every card that falls to your team. Random team, hit draft, or personal box.
