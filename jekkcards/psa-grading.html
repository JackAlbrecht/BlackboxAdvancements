FAQ

Grading questions, answered.

Do I need a PSA or SGC account to submit through JEKK?
No — we submit under JEKK's dealer accounts. You just bring cards in, pick a tier, and pay. We handle all the logistics.
How do I know what declared value to use?
We'll run live eBay and PWCC comps with you at the desk to set fair declared values. Declaring too low can cause a submission bump; too high increases your fee. We aim to land it right.
Can I watch my submission be packed and shipped?
Yes — we send you the cataloged submission sheet with photos before it ships, plus the FedEx tracking number the day it goes out.
What happens if a card comes back a lower grade than I expected?
PSA and SGC grades are final, and we can't control the outcome. If you want to crossover from SGC to PSA (or vice versa), we can submit for a crossover grade with a minimum target.
Do you offer in-house pre-grading?
Yes, informally. Our grading desk will eye your cards and give you a rough pre-grade estimate — centering, surface, edges, corners — before you commit to a submission.
