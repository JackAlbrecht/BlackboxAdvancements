// Grading Services

PSA & SGC
grading

Drop off your raw cards. We handle the submission, the shipping, the tracking, and the return storage. You just come back to pick up your slabs.

Grading a card means sending it to a third-party service (PSA, SGC, BGS) who verify it's authentic, check the condition, and encapsulate it in a slab with a numerical grade from 1 to 10. A PSA-10 of the right card can be worth 10–100x the raw version. Even a clean PSA-9 often doubles the value.

But the submission process is a mess — account setups, forms, bulk orders, shipping, insurance, turnaround times. We run the whole thing for you. You drop the cards off with us. We do the rest.

No PSA account needed. No forms. No shipping logistics. We bulk-submit with our group pricing, track the order, notify you on return, and store your slabs safe until you pick them up. You just dig through your box, pick the hits, and hand them over.

How submissions work.

01

Bring Them In

Walk in with your raw cards — loose, sleeved, penny-sleeved, whatever. We'll look through them with you and pull the ones worth grading.

02

We Submit

We fill out the PSA (or SGC) submission form, bulk package, insure, and ship through our shop account. You get a receipt and a tracker.

03

Pick Up The Slabs

When the return ships back to us, we text you. Come pick up your graded cards — they're already sleeved, stored, and ready.

What we grade.

  • PSA — the industry standard. Bulk, value, and fast-service tiers available.
  • SGC — cleaner turnaround and widely accepted for vintage + modern alike.
  • We accept sports, Pokémon, Magic, One Piece TCG, Lorcana, and most other TCG releases
  • Grading is offered for both walk-in customers and online / shipped-in orders

Turnaround times (typical)

  • PSA Bulk: 45–60 business days
  • PSA Value: 30–45 business days
  • PSA Regular / Express: 10–20 business days (higher fee)
  • SGC: 10–20 business days on most tiers

Pricing varies by PSA/SGC tier and card declared value. We'll go through the math with you in-store — no surprises.

Questions? Call the shop at (360) 303-1789 or send us a message. Better yet — bring the cards in person, we'll sort through them with you on the counter.
