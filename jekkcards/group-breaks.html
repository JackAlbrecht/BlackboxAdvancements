Group breaks are the most fun you can have in the hobby without buying a whole case yourself. You pay for a spot. We rip the wax on camera. Every card that falls to your team is yours. We ship the hits straight to your door.

We run breaks most nights of the week out of the shop. Watch on Instagram Live, come sit at the table in-person, or just check the video later. Either way — the hits are shipped to you within 1–2 business days.

1. Pick a break and pay for your spot. 2. We rip the box on video. 3. Every card that falls to your team goes to you. 4. We ship within 1–2 business days. Simple.

Three ways to break.

01

Random Team

All teams get randomized between the spots. You pay $X per spot, you get every card from the team you randomly land. Equal-price, chance-driven.

02

Hit Draft

Tiered pricing by team. Stars cost more, rebuilders cost less. Pick your team ahead of time, rip the wax, get your hits.

03

Personal Box

You want every single card in the box, not just one team. We rip the box on camera for you and mail you everything — base, parallels, hits, all of it.
