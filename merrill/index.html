Our method

How this goes, without the usual surprise.

The reason most landscape projects go sideways is that nobody agrees on what “done” looks like, and the price moves after work starts. Our process fixes both before we pick up a shovel.

Step i.

A design you approve

We walk the property, sketch a plan, and build a rendering you can actually read. You approve it, in writing, before anything gets dug. If you want to change it, you're doing it on paper, not with a backhoe in the yard.

Step ii.

A fixed price, signed

Not “starting at.” Not “ballpark.” A real number, itemized, signed before work starts. Change orders only happen when you ask for one, and you see the new number before we proceed.

Step iii.

A crew we trained

No subs. No rotating names on your driveway. The same Merrill crew from day one to walkthrough, to seasonal care after. Weekly training is a company policy, not a marketing line.
