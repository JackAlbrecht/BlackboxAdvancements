Sprinkler systems

Installed, repaired, pressure-tested, and put to bed every October.

A sprinkler system is the quietest way to ruin a landscape. A single dead zone browns a corner in two weeks. A stuck valve floods a planting bed. A missed winterization cracks a manifold.

Merrill has been designing, installing, and repairing sprinkler systems in Eastern Idaho for forty-plus years. Every install includes a zone-by-zone pressure test before we hand it back. Every repair includes a walk of the whole system, not just the broken head. And every seasonal contract includes winterization in October, on the calendar, without you calling twice.

  • Seasonal start-up & winterize

Lawn maintenance

A weekly program your neighbors will notice, and ask for.

Mow, edge, blow hardscapes, apply fertilizer on the right week, aerate when the soil opens up, treat for insects before you see them. Trees get their own care plan. Outdoor lighting

The yard you paid for, after the sun goes down.

Low-voltage, professional-grade fixtures. Placed with intent: architectural accents where the house deserves them, path lighting that actually helps at 6am in February, tree uplights that don’t wash out, safety lighting near grade changes and steps. Every circuit is serviceable without digging up a bed.

We do holiday lighting too. Install, maintain, take it down, store it. Most of our lighting clients add holiday to their plan after the first December they don’t have to climb a ladder.
