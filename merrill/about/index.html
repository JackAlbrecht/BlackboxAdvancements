About

Jerry started it in 1981. Tricia and Heath still answer the phone.

Forty-plus years on the same streets, under the same name, with the same standard. Here’s how that has held.

Merrill Quality Landscapes was founded in 1981 by Jerry Merrill on a simple idea: do what you said you would, and be the one to come back when it needs fixing. For twenty years Jerry ran the business out of Rexburg, picking up crews from town and clients one handshake at a time.

When Jerry moved into civic work, he didn’t sell. He handed the company to his daughter Tricia and her husband Heath, who had both grown up around the trucks. They’ve led it ever since — and they’ve done it differently. Not by changing what Jerry built, but by adding training programs, a proper design-and-build studio, robotic mowing, and the systems that make a forty-year company feel like a twenty-person company can still deliver on the Merrill name.

Today we’re a design-build-maintain shop. Weekly internal training is a company rule. Subcontracting is a last resort. The person who walks your property on day one is the person who walks it with you on the day you sign off.

A short history

Forty-plus years, five things that mattered.

  1. 1981

    Jerry Merrill founds the company

    Rexburg, Idaho. One truck, one mower, one rule: do what you said, come back if it breaks.

  2. 2001

    Tricia & Heath take the reins

    Jerry moves into civic work. The second generation steps up without changing the standard.

  3. 2012

    Design studio added in-house

    Full landscape design and build — no more handing plans to outside firms. One roof, one crew.

  4. 2019

    Weed Man regional partnership

    Regional lawn-care partnership. More hands for big seasonal programs, same standard of service.

  5. 2024

    Robotic mowing program

    Robotic mowers added as an option on properties that want one. The person is still the person.

What we mean by “quality”

Three things we don’t negotiate.

One.

Show up

We show up when we said we would. If we’re going to be late, you hear it from us before the appointment, not after. This is the bar.

Two.

Honor the number

The price we signed is the price you pay. If conditions change, we come to you before we change anything — with the new number in writing.

Three.

Stand behind the work

If something we built is wrong, we fix it. That’s not a warranty policy, it’s the thing that has kept us in business for forty-plus years.
