About Mike Winter

Quality work,
since 2008.

Mike Winter started this company on a simple idea: build it like it's your own. Seventeen years and 500+ projects later, that's still the standard. Every deck, every frame, every garage gets the same level of attention — whether it's a bathroom remodel or a full RV barn.

The work speaks for itself: tight joints, plumb walls, square corners, clean job sites. We listen, we plan, we build, and we don't leave until you're happy with the result. That's how a small Olympia contractor turns into the one your neighbor recommends 10 years later.
